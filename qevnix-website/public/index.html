Domain & hosting: customer-owned

Your website assets stay under your control. QEVNIX helps with setup and connection.

Domain name

The customer purchases and owns the domain using their own registrar account and contact details.

  • Customer pays the registrar directly
  • Customer controls renewal and DNS access
  • QEVNIX can help connect the domain to the website

Website hosting

The customer purchases hosting in their own account, unless a different arrangement is agreed in writing.

  • Customer pays the hosting provider directly
  • Hosting plan and renewal are the customer's responsibility
  • QEVNIX can assist with deployment and configuration

Website development & maintenance

These are QEVNIX services and are paid to QEVNIX as per the agreed quotation or selected monthly plan.

  • Development payment follows agreed milestones
  • Optional maintenance is billed monthly
  • Maintenance does not include domain or hosting renewal fees

If a customer asks QEVNIX to purchase a domain or hosting on their behalf, this must be agreed in writing in advance. Third-party charges will be shown separately and receipts/account ownership arrangements will be shared with the customer.
